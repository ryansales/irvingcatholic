Know a place that belongs
on the map?

Parishes and chapels, schools, seminaries and religious houses, ministries, Catholic businesses, and businesses owned by Catholics — if it's in Irving, we want it listed. Every listing is free, and always will be.

Fill this out and it lands in one inbox for review. Nothing goes live automatically; a real person reads each one, checks the details, and adds it — usually within a week or two.

Two ways to send it. Use the form below, or just email everything to ryan@salesfamily.net — photos as attachments is perfectly fine. There's a copy-and-paste checklist at the bottom of this page if you'd rather write an email.

Irving only. This directory covers the City of Irving — including Las Colinas and Valley Ranch. Wonderful places in Dallas, Grand Prairie, Coppell, and Farmers Branch are out of scope, and we do have to say no to them.
