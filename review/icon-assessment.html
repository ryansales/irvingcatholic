Geocoding

The coordinates are in, and one building needed a correction

All ten addresses resolved through the US Census geocoder and all ten landed inside the Irving city limits. They live in review/list-1-physical-irving.csv, with paste-ready listing objects in review/list-1-geocoded.js.

511 E John Carpenter Ste 500 was wrong three different ways. The Census geocoder returned two points 263 m apart for the one building, because the two rows spell the street differently — "John W Carpenter" against "John Carpenter" — and a street-centreline geocoder follows the spelling. The coordinate the site already carried for Beatitudes and the SVdP office, 32.884692, -96.949608, turned out to be one of the approximate placeholders the README warns about: 3 km from the real door. All four listings now sit at 32.860489, -96.934850, confirmed against Google Maps.

Two rows now read manual in the Geocoder column. That is a convention geocode.py respects: a manual row is never re-geocoded, and its coordinate is what every other row at the same address gets. Hand-checked pins survive a re-run, and addresses are matched on a normalised key so one building can no longer collect two pins.

To re-run it after an address changes: Actions tab → Geocode List 1 addresses → Run workflow, then open the run and click Open the pull request in the summary. The job cannot push to main itself — repository rules require a pull request — so it puts results on their own branch and links the form.
